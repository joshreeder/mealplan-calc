Heads up: Seattle U meal plans run on quarters, and any leftover balance carries automatically into the next quarter, up to $250 (the rollover account itself caps at $250 too — you use up the new quarter's plan before dipping into it). Anything left at the end of spring quarter is forfeited and does not carry into the next academic year. So the goal is to land near $0 by the end of spring, not necessarily each quarter.
